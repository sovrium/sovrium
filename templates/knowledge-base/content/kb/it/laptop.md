# Setting up your laptop

Your hardware, your accounts and the security basics, in the order you meet them
on day one.

![A laptop on a desk, its screen showing the handbook beside the password vault](/handbook/laptop-setup.avif)

## Hardware

IT assigns each colleague a laptop from the asset register. If something is
broken or missing, open a ticket with IT — do not buy a replacement yourself.

## Accounts

| Service        | How you get access              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Email          | Created before your first day   |
| Password vault | An invitation from IT on day 1  |
| Code hosting   | Ask your team lead              |
| Internal apps  | Sign in with your company email |

## Security basics

- Full-disk encryption stays on. It ships enabled; leave it that way.
- The password vault generates and keeps every password — none in plain text.
- Updates install weekly. Restart when prompted, not later in the month.

The full rules are in the [security policy](/kb/policies/security-policy).
