# Sources — design, UX, states, copy and accessibility

Everything in `design-plan.md`, `states-and-forms.md`, `copy.md`, `anti-slop.md`,
`accessibility.md`, `data-dense.md` and the critique section of `SKILL.md` is written in this
skill's own words. Nothing is copied verbatim from a copyrighted source.

**Licence classes**

- **Paraphrase only** — commercial or all-rights-reserved works. Ideas are restated in new words and attributed; no sentence, table or list is reproduced: Nielsen Norman Group articles, Baymard Institute, Refactoring UI, Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Podmajersky's _Strategic Writing for UX_, and any other source below without a stated licence.
- **Permissive** — adapted with attribution under the stated licence: Vercel web-interface-guidelines (MIT), Impeccable (Apache-2.0), Anthropic `frontend-design` skill (Apache-2.0), Google DESIGN.md (Apache-2.0), OneRedOak design-review (MIT), Carbon Design System (Apache-2.0), Primer (MIT), GOV.UK Design System (OGL v3 for content, MIT for code), Material Design (CC BY 4.0).
- **Standards** — W3C documents are cited by criterion number; their wording is not reproduced.

URLs were fetched or returned by search on 2026-09-24. A site-level URL that remains below had no verified page URL.
