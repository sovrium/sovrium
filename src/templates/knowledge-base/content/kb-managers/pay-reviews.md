# Pay reviews

Pay is reviewed once a year, in October, for everyone at the same time.

## Before the review

Read each person's year against their role: what they owned, what changed. Write
it down in five lines or fewer.

## The decision

Proposals go to Hannah Okafor by 15 October. Changes are decided within the
salary bands and apply from 1 January.

## Telling your team

Tell each person their outcome yourself, before the letter arrives, and explain
it in the same terms you wrote it down.
