# Hiring a colleague

From an open role to a signed offer, in four steps.

## Open the role

Write the role in two paragraphs — what the person will own in their first six
months, and who they will work with — and send it to Hannah Okafor.

## Interview

Two interviews and one piece of real work, never more. Each interviewer writes
their notes before talking to the others.

## Decide

The hiring manager decides, the same week as the last interview. Tell every
candidate the outcome, in writing, whatever it is.

## Make the offer

Hannah prepares the offer from the salary bands. It goes out within two working
days of the decision.
