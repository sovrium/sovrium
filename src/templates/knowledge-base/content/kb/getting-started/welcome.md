# Welcome

This is how Corvel works, written down. When a question has been answered once,
the answer lives here, with the name of the person who owns it.

## How it is organised

- **Getting started** — your first week, your accounts, who to ask.
- **IT** — your laptop, your access and the security basics.
- **Policies** — expenses, security and the other rules we agreed on.

## When something is out of date

Tell the owner named at the foot of the article. Each article is a plain
markdown file under `content/kb/`; the owner changes the file, and the handbook
shows the new version on the next deploy.
