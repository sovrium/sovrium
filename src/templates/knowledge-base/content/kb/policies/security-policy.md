# Security policy

Security is everyone's job, and the rules are deliberately few and firm.

## The rules

1. **Passwords live in the vault.** Never in files, chats or a shared browser.
2. **Two-factor everywhere it is offered.** No exception for a quick account.
3. **Lock your screen** when you step away — at home too.
4. **Report an incident straight away.** A false alarm costs minutes; a hidden
   incident costs weeks. Nobody is blamed for reporting.
5. **Company data stays on company tools.** No work document forwarded to a
   personal account or to a tool IT has not approved.

## Phishing

When an email feels off — urgency, an odd sender, an attachment you did not
expect — do not click. Forward it to IT and delete it. Nobody is ever faulted
for checking twice.
