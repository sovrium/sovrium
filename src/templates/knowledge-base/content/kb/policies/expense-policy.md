# Expense policy

Spend the company's money like it is your own, keep the receipt, file it the
same week.

## What's covered

- Travel to customer sites and conferences (economy or second class).
- Meals while travelling, within reason.
- Software and hardware your manager approved beforehand.
- Books and courses for your work — no approval needed under €50.

::: callout type="warning"
**Past its review date.** Finance reviews this policy every six months; the last
check was 12 March. Until it is checked again, confirm a large expense with Dan
Whitfield in Finance first.
:::

## How to file

1. Photograph the receipt the day you get it.
2. File it in the expenses app with the right category.
3. An admin approves it — you get an email either way.
